100% Free, Local C# Heuristic & Collaborative Engine

CanteenHub operates without paid third-party AI APIs or external cloud costs. Recommendations are computed locally in real-time by analyzing:

  • Time-of-Day Relevance: Automatically detects breakfast (06:00-11:30), lunch (11:30-15:30), snacks (15:30-19:00), and dinner (19:00-23:30).
  • Basket Association Mining: Suggests cross-sell items ("Complete Your Meal") based on customer order co-occurrences.
  • Portion Availability Gating: Automatically suppresses items when kitchen raw ingredient stock is insufficient to cook a portion.
  • Privacy First: Anonymous session telemetry with zero customer PII leakage or tracking cookies.
